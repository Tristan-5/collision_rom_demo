Cascade Prototype Notes (Experimental)

- Purpose: Minimal shell-model cascade to explore energy transfer across scales.

- Observations:
  - Energy spectrum (log–log) decreases with shell index → scale separation visible.
  - Total energy increases over time leads to forcing exceeds dissipation, no steady state yet.

- Parameters used:
  - N = 10 shells  
  - dt = 0.001, tmax = 50  
  - forcing = 0.1, nu = 0.001  
  - small random initial conditions for dynamics

- Interpretation:
  - Prototype successfully demonstrates energy injection and distribution across shells.  
  - Shows minimal cascade behavior.  
  - Still simplified; does not capture intermittency or steady-state turbulence.
